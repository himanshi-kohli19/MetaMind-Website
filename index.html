FAQ

Questions, answered without the ad-tech fog.

Metamind is an independent AI agent for audience discovery and controlled Meta ad management.

What is an audience site?

A useful branded experience—such as a guide, calculator, or offer—that helps a visitor and provides signals about intent. Its role is discovery and learning, not replacing the business’s product.

How does Metamind identify a stronger audience?

It compares observed real-product conversions over a defined period, subject to enough data and attribution limits. A stronger observed rate is useful evidence, not proof of causation.

Can I review actions first?

Yes. With automation disabled, routine changes become suggestions. Consequential actions—such as a first publication, domain purchase, new campaign, budget increase, optimisation change, or client report delivery—always require approval.

Will it work for every business?

Not identically. Event availability, category restrictions, data volume, attribution, and platform eligibility affect what can be measured, optimised, or used for audiences and lookalikes.

Is Metamind affiliated with Meta?

No. Metamind is an independent product and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Meta.
